Lantern

Honest health grades for Costco food, in the aisle.

SAB CDF

Scan a barcode and land in that product's world: a forest whose health mirrors the food's. S is lush and alive; each grade down loses colour and life until F is bare woodland in haze. Every grade shows its evidence — what's confirmed, what's only reported, what's disputed — with sources you can read.

How grades work

Four dimensions, weighted: nutrition, additives, processing, packaging. Any confirmed high-severity concern caps a product at D. Grades are an aid to reading a label, not medical advice.
